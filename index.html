Why “actual size” matters

A 12-inch shelf fitting the wall is hard to picture from a number. So this site draws the measurement at 100% actual size on your screen, using the same pixel math as a physical ruler.

Every ruler page ships with a one-time screen calibration — hold any bank card against the on-screen card, save, and every ruler on the site becomes true to life on your exact display. Below each ruler you get ten real-world comparisons chosen for that length, from credit cards at the small end to door widths and towels at the large end.

How long is an inch, really? An inch has been defined internationally as exactly 2.54 centimeters since 1959, and everything on this site is computed from that definition — no estimates, no rounded screenshots.
