How long is 12 inches compared to an object?

Standard Wooden Ruler

About 12 inches

A 12-inch wooden ruler is the standard school ruler, a foot long from end to end. The foot was fixed at 12 inches long before the inch itself was pinned to 2.54 cm.

2

Classic 33 RPM Vinyl LP

About 12 inches

A 12-inch LP holds about 20 minutes of audio per side and spins at 33 rpm. 12 inches is the standard vinyl diameter, larger than the 7-inch single.

3

Standard Footlong Submarine Sandwich

About 12 inches

A footlong sub is 12 inches end to end, which is where the name comes from. The roll is baked fresh and cut along its length.

4

Large 12-Inch Delivery Pizza

About 12 inches

A 12-inch pizza cuts into eight slices and is a medium at most chains. That size feeds two people rather than a whole family.

5

12-Inch Cast Iron Skillet

About 12 inches

A 12-inch cast iron skillet is the largest pan that still fits a standard home burner. It sears well and survives decades of use.

6

12-Inch Traffic Safety Cone

About 12 inches

A 12-inch traffic cone is the compact roadside size, used for marking hazards rather than closing lanes. Taller 28-inch and 36-inch cones do that job.

7

12-Inch Hacksaw Blade

About 12 inches

A 12-inch hacksaw frame is a standard length for cutting steel pipe and thin stock. The blade is the consumable part and needs tensioning before use.

8

IKEA KALLAX Storage Box

About 12 inches

KALLAX shelves take dividers that slot into 13-inch bays, so a 12-inch box leaves a little clearance. The frames are sold unfinished for painting or staining.

9

12-Inch Stainless Whisk

About 12 inches

A 12-inch balloon whisk is a standard size for a home kitchen. The wires hold air in egg whites and cream, and the handle reaches the base of a wide pan.

10

12-Inch Wooden Macrame Dowel

About 12 inches

A 12-inch hardwood dowel is a common start for macrame wall hangings. The wood is smooth enough for cord to grip without splitting the fibres.

Frequently asked questions about 12 inches

Why are there 12 inches in a foot?

The division descends from Roman measuring practice, where a foot was split into 12 unciae — the same root that gave us the word "ounce." It has been fixed at 12 far longer than the inch itself has been pinned to 2.54 centimeters.

Is 12 inches exactly one foot?

Yes, by definition. It is also the length of a standard school ruler, which is why this page doubles as a foot reference.

How many centimeters is 12 inches?

30.48 centimeters. School rulers labeled "30 cm" are actually about half a centimeter short of a true foot.

What is measured in 12-inch units every day?

Pizzas (12 inches is a medium at most chains), vinyl LPs (12 inches across), and the original "footlong" sandwiches — this is the inch length people meet most often.
